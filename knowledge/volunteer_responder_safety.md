# Prototype Volunteer and Responder Safety Guidance

This prototype knowledge material is generic operational guidance, not a substitute for trained supervision or official safety requirements.

Assign volunteers according to verified skills, local conditions, and clear supervision. Brief each team on its objective, hazards, check-in method, escalation contact, and stop-work authority. Never ask volunteers to enter floodwater, unstable buildings, fire zones, or other high-risk environments without proper training, equipment, and authorization.

Use buddy systems where feasible, record team deployments, and schedule rest, hydration, and welfare checks. Volunteers should report injuries, lost contact, unsafe conditions, and unmet needs immediately. Pause or reassign work when conditions become unsafe.
