# Prototype Earthquake Response Guidance

This prototype knowledge material is generic operational guidance, not an official government protocol. Local emergency authorities determine safe actions for a specific event.

During shaking, protect people from falling objects and avoid windows. After shaking stops, move to a safer open area only when it can be done without entering damaged structures or hazardous zones. Expect aftershocks and keep response teams briefed on changing conditions.

Report trapped people, visible structural damage, fires, gas smells, injuries, and blocked access routes to the appropriate emergency coordinator. Do not allow untrained responders to enter compromised buildings. Establish a visible assembly point, account for people, and prioritize urgent medical triage.
