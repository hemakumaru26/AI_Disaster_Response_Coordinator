# Prototype Emergency Medical and First-Response Guidance

This prototype knowledge material is generic and does not replace trained medical care, dispatch instructions, or local emergency procedures.

Call or coordinate with emergency medical services for life-threatening conditions. Use trained personnel for first aid and triage whenever available. Prioritize scene safety before approaching injured people, and do not move a seriously injured person unless there is an immediate danger.

Record the location, number of patients, apparent severity, hazards, and access constraints when requesting support. Keep patient information limited to what is necessary for coordination and protect privacy. Arrange transport only through appropriate, safe channels.
