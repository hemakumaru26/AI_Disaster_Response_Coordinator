# Prototype Flood Response Guidance

This prototype knowledge material provides generic operational guidance, not an official government protocol. Confirm local authority instructions before acting.

Prioritize life safety. Identify immediate hazards such as fast-moving water, submerged electrical equipment, damaged roads, and isolated people. Do not direct responders or evacuees to enter floodwater unless they are trained, equipped, and authorized.

Share a clear location, the number of people affected, urgent medical needs, and safe access routes with the incident coordinator. Move people from low-lying areas toward safe, identified locations. Keep evacuation groups together where possible and record vulnerable people who may need extra support.

At shelters, track arrivals, available space, drinking water, sanitation, medication needs, and family reunification information. Reassess conditions when rainfall, water level, or access routes change.
