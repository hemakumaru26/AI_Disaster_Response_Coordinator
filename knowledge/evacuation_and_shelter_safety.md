# Prototype Evacuation and Shelter Safety Guidance

This prototype knowledge material is generic and must be adapted to local authority directions and the actual shelter capacity data in the application.

Choose evacuation routes that avoid known hazards and communicate alternatives in case a route closes. Give clear instructions about destination, transport, assembly time, and what essential items people should bring when feasible. Provide additional assistance for children, older adults, people with disabilities, and people requiring medication.

At a shelter, verify available capacity before directing people there. Keep entrances, exits, access lanes, and emergency routes clear. Record occupancy, basic needs, medical concerns, and protection concerns. Monitor water, sanitation, ventilation, food, and crowding; escalate when safe capacity or basic conditions cannot be maintained.
