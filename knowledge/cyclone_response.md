# Prototype Cyclone Response Guidance

This prototype knowledge material is generic preparedness and response guidance, not an official government protocol. Follow directions from local disaster-management authorities.

Before severe winds arrive, communicate evacuation routes and safe shelter locations, secure loose equipment when it is safe to do so, and identify people who require transport or medical support. Avoid relying on power, mobile coverage, or roads that may become unavailable.

During the event, keep people away from windows, damaged structures, coastlines, flood-prone roads, and downed power lines. Do not deploy volunteers into unsafe wind conditions. Maintain regular welfare checks and record urgent requests for rescue or medical support.

After the event, assess hazards before reopening routes or facilities. Treat damaged electrical lines, unstable buildings, contaminated water, and blocked roads as potential hazards until qualified personnel confirm safety.
